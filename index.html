Oracle iExpense Approval Assistant

Determine your expense approval chain before submission

How to Use This Tool

  1. Select your Operating Unit (CCP, CCH, or CCF)
  2. Choose your Expense Template from the dropdown
  3. Enter the Total Amount of your expense
  4. Provide additional information if prompted (grant fund status, submitter name)
  5. Click "Get Approval Chain" to see who will approve your expense

Note: This tool provides approval information only. It does not submit expenses or perform calculations.

Common Questions

Q: What if I don't know my Operating Unit?

Contact your department administrator or check your expense report submission history in Oracle iExpense.

Q: Can this tool submit my expense report?

No, this tool only provides approval chain information. You must submit your expense through Oracle iExpense.

Q: What are grant funds (CCH)?

Grant funds are research or project-specific funding with codes 42000 or 41235. Check with your department if unsure.

Q: Where can I find mileage or per diem rates?

Mileage Rates: DOD Mileage Rates
Per Diem Rates: GSA Per Diem Rates
